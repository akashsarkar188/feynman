## Summary
This draft provides a useful, bounded synthesis of value shifts in planning history, organized into a five-phase periodization and mapped to canonical theory strands (rational-comprehensive, incrementalist, advocacy, communicative, and justice/postcolonial). It is strongest where it explicitly scopes confidence and flags access limitations.

As a verification pass, however, the main risk is evidence overreach from partial-access sources and a few internal inconsistencies in what was supposedly removed vs retained. Several high-level claims are plausible but not fully auditable from the cited artifacts (many are metadata/landing pages rather than quoted full text). The paper is close to credible as a *scoping memo*, but not yet robust as a citation-secure literature review.
