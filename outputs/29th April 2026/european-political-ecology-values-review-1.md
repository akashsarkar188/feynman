## Summary
This draft provides a concise narrative review of “values” in European political ecology and organizes the field into five value clusters (justice, care, commons, relational, decolonial), then maps these onto policy domains and method patterns. It also includes transparent caveats about partial access and absence of meta-analysis.

As a verification/audit pass, the main risk is not overclaiming novelty but **evidence integrity**: several high-level synthesis claims are stated with strong language while a substantial subset of cited sources is access-limited in this run. The draft is close to usable, but a few claims need tightening or better support to avoid reviewer pushback.

## Strengths
- [S1] The draft explicitly discloses evidence limitations in “Evidence caveats” and “Verification note,” which is good audit hygiene.
- [S2] It avoids fabricated quantification and explicitly states “No quantitative meta-analysis was run,” reducing risk of false precision.
- [S3] It already flags two single-source-sensitive areas ([8], [10]), which is exactly the right risk framing.

## Weaknesses

- [W2] **MAJOR:** Some “consensus” statements present field-wide agreement without showing contradictory literature or scope boundaries (possible over-generalization).
- [W3] **MAJOR:** The “Thematic evidence by domain” section implies comparative evidentiary strength (“Strong critique…”, “increasingly operationalized…”) but does not show a traceable evidence matrix.
