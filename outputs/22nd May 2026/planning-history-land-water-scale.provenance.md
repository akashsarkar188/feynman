## Notes
- The final review uses a planning-history lens: it emphasizes historical institutions, path dependence, transnational planning histories, and the long-run production of land-water territories.
- For follow-up work, the most promising extension is a narrower synthesis focused only on **North Sea / Rhine / Elbe / Scheldt cases** or a second pass that compares **ports vs deltas vs cross-border basins** as distinct scale-making histories.
