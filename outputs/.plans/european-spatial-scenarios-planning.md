# Plan: Spatial Scenarios in European Spatial Planning

- **Slug:** `european-spatial-scenarios-planning`
- **Date:** 2026-04-28
- **Objective:** Produce a literature review focused on how spatial scenarios are used in spatial planning across European contexts (EU, national, regional, metropolitan).

## Scope and key questions

1. How are “spatial scenarios” defined and operationalized in European spatial planning literature?
2. Which methodological families dominate in Europe (participatory scenario planning, geodesign, LUCC/CA/ABM, integrated assessment, backcasting)?
3. In which planning domains are scenarios applied (land-use, climate adaptation, transport, biodiversity/NBS, regional development)?
4. What evidence exists for practical uptake in planning processes and policy implementation in Europe?
5. What barriers recur (institutional fit, data interoperability, governance fragmentation, uncertainty communication)?
6. What do European studies identify as best practices for planning-relevant scenario workflows?

## Source strategy

- **Papers:** alphaXiv/arXiv plus web-retrieved journal pages, review papers, and case studies.
- **Policy/official documents:** EU-level planning/adaptation/scenario guidance where needed to ground “practice” claims.
- **Code/repos:** only when required to assess reproducibility/implementation claims.
