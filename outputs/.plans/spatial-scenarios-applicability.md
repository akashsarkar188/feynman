## Objective
Produce a concise literature review on what "spatial scenarios" means across the research literature, which methodological families dominate, where they are applied, and what evidence exists about their practical applicability and limitations.

## Scope and framing
Because the topic phrase is broad and under-specified, this review will treat **spatial scenarios** as scenario-based methods whose outputs, assumptions, or evaluation are explicitly spatial (e.g., land-use planning, urban futures, socio-environmental assessment, energy systems with spatial aggregation, participatory spatial Delphi).

## Key questions
1. How is "spatial scenarios" defined across the literature?
2. Which methodological families recur most often?
3. In which domains are spatial scenarios applied?
4. What kinds of evidence are used to justify applicability (case studies, simulations, participatory planning, forecast skill, policy uptake)?
5. What limitations and disagreements appear across the literature?
6. What open methodological gaps remain?
