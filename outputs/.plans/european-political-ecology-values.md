# Plan: Values in European Political Ecology

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Topic:** values in european political ecology
- **Slug:** `european-political-ecology-values`

## 1) Scope and key questions

### Core research questions
1. How are "values" conceptualized in European political ecology scholarship (e.g., justice, care, commons, degrowth, relational values, pluriversality)?
2. Which empirical domains dominate (energy transitions, biodiversity/conservation, agriculture/food systems, urban political ecology, extraction frontiers)?
3. What theoretical lineages are most influential (political ecology, environmental justice, ecological economics, feminist political ecology, decolonial thought)?
4. What methods are used to study values (discourse analysis, ethnography, participatory methods, Q-method, valuation studies)?
5. Where do disagreements appear (normative universals vs. plural values, Anthropocene governance vs. grassroots autonomy, reformist vs. transformative pathways)?
6. What evidence exists on policy uptake in European governance (EU Green Deal, biodiversity strategy, just transition mechanisms)?

### Boundaries
- Geographic focus: Europe (EU + UK + EEA/selected non-EU Europe when relevant).
- Time focus: primarily 2014–2026, with canonical pre-2014 anchors where needed.
- Literature type: peer-reviewed papers/books chapters from major journals in political ecology, environmental governance, EJ, degrowth.

## 2) Source strategy

- **Papers (primary):** alphaXiv/semantic search + targeted paper Q&A for key texts.
- **Web (current policy context):** official EU/EEA policy docs and institutional reports, plus recent synthesis articles.
- **Repos/code:** likely low relevance; only used if value-mapping methods/tools are software-backed.
