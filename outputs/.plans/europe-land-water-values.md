# Plan: Values in History of Spatial Transformation in Land-Water Interaction in Europe

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Topic:** values in history of spatial transformation in land water interaction in Europe
- **Slug:** `europe-land-water-values`

## 1) Scope and key questions

### Core research questions
1. What were the dominant values driving historical land-water transformations in Europe (e.g., control, efficiency, economic gain, flood protection, navigation, aesthetics, ecological integrity)?
2. How have these values changed over time, across different historical periods (e.g., pre-industrial, industrialization, post-WWII reconstruction, environmental era, climate adaptation era)?
3. What role did specific actors (e.g., engineers, state agencies, local communities, landowners, environmental movements) play in shaping and contesting these values?
4. How have scientific and technical advancements (e.g., hydraulic engineering, ecological science) influenced value prioritization and management approaches in land-water systems?
5. What are the regional differences in value evolution across Europe, reflecting diverse socio-economic, political, and ecological contexts?
6. How have values related to ecological functions (e.g., biodiversity, ecosystem services) emerged and integrated into land-water management history?

### Boundaries
- Geographic focus: Europe (broadly defined, including regional variations within and across nation-states).
- Time frame: primarily from the early modern period (approx. 16th century) to the present (2026), with a focus on major shifts and turning points.
- Literature type: peer-reviewed papers, book chapters, and historical monographs from disciplines like environmental history, historical geography, planning history, and water management studies.

## 2) Source strategy

- **Papers/books (primary):** alphaXiv/semantic search for environmental history, historical geography, water management history in Europe. Targeted search for specific river basins or regions.
- **Web (current policy context):** official EU/EEA directives (e.g., Water Framework Directive, Flood Directive) for recent value integration, if relevant to historical shifts.
- **Repos/code:** likely low relevance.
